Must-see sights 景点

Nanjing Massacre Memorial Hall

侵华日军南京大屠杀遇难同胞纪念馆

The Memorial Hall of the Victims in Nanjing Massacre by Japanese Invaders commemorates the victims of the 1937 massacre. A place for remembrance and reflection, with historical evidence and survivor testimony. Allow 2-3 hours; exhibits contain distressing images and human remains.

Visiting: Free entry with advance real-name booking through the official WeChat account. Reserve 1-7 days ahead and bring the original passport or ID used to book. Exhibition halls require separate reservations.

Regular hours: 08:30-17:30, last admission 16:30. Closed Mondays except statutory holidays. Address: 418 Shuiximen Street; Metro Line 2, Yunjin Road.

Mid-December caution: December 13 is the national memorial day. Special closures may affect visits around it. December 2026 arrangements are not yet verified: check current official notices before fixing this day.

Must-try food 美食

Salted duck

盐水鸭

The signature dish. Cold, tender, lightly salted. Try it at a local duck shop.

Signature

Duck blood and vermicelli soup

鸭血粉丝汤

Glass noodles in a clear duck broth with duck blood, tofu and offal. Warming in December.

Your itinerary 5-13 December 2026

Driving-time guide: Each estimate below starts independently at Deji Plaza, 18 Zhongshan Road, Xinjiekou, not at the previous stop. Rounded planning ranges are based on current map routes checked 7 October 2026, with a margin for ordinary traffic. They are not December traffic forecasts. Allow extra time for parking, security, pedestrian streets, scenic-area shuttles and walking. Confirm the route in Amap or Baidu Maps locally before setting off.

Sat 5 Dec: Shopping and the old city

Visit Deji Plaza (德基广场), then explore the Qinhuai riverside at Fuzimiao (夫子庙秦淮河) and Laomendong historic street (老门东). The riverside is a good evening stop; leave time to explore the pedestrian streets on foot.

From Deji: Deji Plaza - 0 min; Fuzimiao/Qinhuai riverside - 15-30 min (about 3.7 km); Laomendong - 20-35 min (about 5.1 km).

Sun 6 Dec: Purple Mountain

Visit Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum (中山陵) and Meiling Palace (美龄宫). Keep the two sights together in the Purple Mountain area. Allow for steps, walking and any shuttle transfer; check current reservations before visiting.

From Deji: Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum - 35-50 min (about 13.3 km); Meiling Palace - 25-40 min (about 10.3 km). Road arrival does not include the walk to the mausoleum.

Mon 7 Dec: Travel to Yangzhou

Travel to Yangzhou (扬州). Keep the rest of the day flexible for arrival and exploring; no specific Yangzhou hotel or attraction has been fixed.

From Deji: Yangzhou city centre - 1 hr 30 min-2 hr (about 100 km by road). The exact time depends on your hotel or destination, bridge traffic and stops.

Tue 8 Dec: Return to Nanjing

Travel back from Yangzhou to Nanjing. Leave the remainder of the day flexible.

Return drive: Yangzhou city centre to Deji Plaza - 1 hr 30 min-2 hr; confirm again once the Yangzhou starting point is known.

Wed 9 Dec: Dinner with Lucas

Dinner with Lucas. Keep the daytime flexible.

From Deji: Driving time TBC until the dinner venue is chosen.

Thu 10 Dec: Nanjing Massacre Memorial Hall

Visit the Nanjing Massacre Memorial Hall (侵华日军南京大屠杀遇难同胞纪念馆). Allow 2-3 hours and time for reflection afterwards.

From Deji: 20-35 min (about 5.2 km), plus entry checks.

Provisional - opening must be confirmed. December 13 is the national memorial day, and special closures can affect December 10 too. December 2026 arrangements are not yet verified. Check official notices and the official WeChat account before fixing this visit. If open, free entry requires real-name booking 1-7 days ahead and the original passport/ID used for booking. Keep an alternative day available. Full visitor guidance above.

Fri 11 Dec: Niushoushan and Hongjuesi Tower

Visit Niushoushan (牛首山), including Hongjuesi Tower / Hongjue Temple Pagoda (弘觉寺塔). The historic pagoda is distinct from the newer Foding Tower. Allow time for the park's internal travel and walking.

From Deji: Niushoushan Cultural Tourism Zone entrance at 18 Ningdan Avenue - 45-65 min (about 22.7 km). This is the road transfer to the park, not a drive to the pagoda itself.

Sat 12 Dec: Lake, temple and Ming city wall

Visit Xuanwu Lake (玄武湖), Jiming Temple (鸡鸣寺) and the Ming city wall (明城墙). The Taicheng/Jiefang Gate section is the suggested wall entry for this lake-and-temple day; the wall has several entrances.

From Deji: Xuanwu Lake via Xuanwu Gate - 15-25 min (about 3.2 km); Jiming Temple - 15-25 min (about 3.5 km); Taicheng/Jiefang Gate city wall - 15-25 min (about 3.2 km). These are separate drives from Deji, not three consecutive legs.

Sun 13 Dec: Return to Singapore

Return to Singapore. Flight and departure-airport details are not yet included.

Possible airport transfer: If departing from Nanjing Lukou International Airport, allow roughly 45-65 min driving from Deji (about 42 km), in addition to your airline's required check-in time. Confirm the airport and flight first; memorial-day traffic controls may add time.

Tips

  • Mid-December is cold: typically around 1°C at night to 10°C during the day. These are long-term averages, not a forecast; temperatures can dip below 0°C. Bring a warm coat, gloves and a scarf.
  • Book popular sights ahead (the Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum often needs reservations; check current rules).
  • Check opening days before you go. Many museums close one day a week.
  • For the Memorial Hall, confirm December 2026 opening notices before making firm plans, especially around December 13. Keep voices low and follow the on-site photography rules.
  • Hours, ticket rules and prices change. Confirm them close to the trip.
