Must-see sights 景点

Nanjing Massacre Memorial Hall

侵华日军南京大屠杀遇难同胞纪念馆

The Memorial Hall of the Victims in Nanjing Massacre by Japanese Invaders commemorates the victims of the 1937 massacre. A place for remembrance and reflection, with historical evidence and survivor testimony. Allow 2-3 hours; exhibits contain distressing images and human remains.

Visiting: Free entry with advance real-name booking through the official WeChat account. Reserve 1-7 days ahead and bring the original passport or ID used to book. Exhibition halls require separate reservations.

Regular hours: 08:30-17:30, last admission 16:30. Closed Mondays except statutory holidays. Address: 418 Shuiximen Street; Metro Line 2, Yunjin Road.

Mid-December caution: December 13 is the national memorial day. Special closures may affect visits around it. December 2026 arrangements are not yet verified: check current official notices before fixing this day.

Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum

中山陵

Hillside memorial on Purple Mountain with a long stairway up. Go early; booking ahead is usually needed.

Purple Mountain

Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum

明孝陵

Tomb of the Hongwu Emperor, reached by the Sacred Way lined with stone animals.

UNESCO site

Confucius Temple (Fuzimiao)

夫子庙

Temple complex by the Qinhuai River. Best after dark when the lanterns are lit, and full of snack stalls.

Presidential Palace

总统府

Former seat of the Republic of China government, with gardens and museum halls.

Xuanwu Lake

Large city lake park with islands and views of the old city wall. Good for a slow walk.

Must-try food 美食

Salted duck

盐水鸭

The signature dish. Cold, tender, lightly salted. Try it at a local duck shop.

Signature

Duck blood and vermicelli soup

鸭血粉丝汤

Glass noodles in a clear duck broth with duck blood, tofu and offal. Warming in December.

Winter favorite

Tangbao soup dumplings

汤包

Juicy steamed dumplings, usually eaten with vinegar and ginger. Bite a small hole and sip the soup first.

Breakfast
酥饼

Duck oil pancake

鸭油酥烧饼

Flaky baked pastry made with duck fat, often sold at street stalls.

Street snack
梅花糕

Plum blossom cakes

梅花糕

Small sweet cakes cooked in flower-shaped molds with fillings, found around Fuzimiao.

Dessert

Tea eggs and Qinhuai snacks

秦淮小吃

Fuzimiao and the Qinhuai area have a wide range of small eats, good for grazing in the evening.

Sample plan edit me

Day 1: Old city

Arrive, Presidential Palace; add Zhonghua Gate / City Wall only if time allows. Evening at Confucius Temple and Qinhuai River. Dinner: salted duck.

Day 2: Purple Mountain

Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum, Ming Xiaoling, Linggu Temple. Duck blood vermicelli soup for lunch.

Day 3: Remembrance and a quiet walk

Nanjing Massacre Memorial Hall in the morning, subject to reservation and opening notices. Allow 2-3 hours and time to reflect afterwards. If you feel ready, take a gentle walk by Xuanwu Lake later. Move this day if it falls on a Monday or a special closure.

Day 4: Museum

Nanjing Museum in the morning, last snacks, depart.

Tips

  • Mid-December is cold: typically around 1°C at night to 10°C during the day. These are long-term averages, not a forecast; temperatures can dip below 0°C. Bring a warm coat, gloves and a scarf.
  • Book popular sights ahead (the Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum often needs reservations; check current rules).
  • Check opening days before you go. Many museums close one day a week.
  • For the Memorial Hall, confirm December 2026 opening notices before making firm plans, especially around December 13. Keep voices low and follow the on-site photography rules.
  • Hours, ticket rules and prices change. Confirm them close to the trip.
